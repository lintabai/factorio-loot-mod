---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-05-24
  Features:
    - Initial release. Diablo/PoE-style rarity and affix system for crafted entities and armor.
    - Five rarity tiers (Normal/Magic/Rare/Epic/Legendary) gated by research.
    - 9 currency orbs for rerolling: Transmutation, Alteration, Alchemy, Chaos, Augmentation,
      Annulment, Scouring, Exaltation, Blessed.
    - Tinkerer's Forge: custom container with side panel GUI showing destruction risk.
    - Affix pools for assemblers (incl. resource-specific), miners, furnaces, inserters,
      electric poles, labs (incl. science-type-specific), armor.
    - Lucky/Cursed armor modifier shifts rarity rolls.
    - "of the Artificer" armor suffix grants +1 rarity tier when hand-crafting.
    - Biter-Attracting lab affix spawns biters on research completion.
    - 13 research nodes including separate paths for orb crafting and affix mastery.
    - Multiplayer-safe state management via unit_number-keyed storage.
